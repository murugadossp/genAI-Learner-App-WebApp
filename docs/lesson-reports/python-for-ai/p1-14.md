## 2. Curriculum alignment

- **Learner outcome:** Filter rows of a pandas DataFrame by creating a boolean mask (`df["tokens"] > 20`), applying it with `df[mask]` or inline `df[df["tokens"] > 20]`, and combining simple conditions with `&` and parentheses—while knowing that filtering returns a new DataFrame, preserves original row index labels, and leaves the original `df` unchanged.
- **Misconception addressed:**
  1. Thinking `df["tokens"] > 20` returns matching rows directly instead of a 1D boolean `Series` (mask).
  2. Using Python `and` between two Series (raises `ValueError`) or omitting parentheses around comparisons with `&` (raises `TypeError` due to operator precedence).
  3. Assuming `df[mask]` modifies `df` in place.
  4. Expecting filtered rows to automatically renumber their index starting from `0`.
- **Prerequisites and adjacent lessons:** Prerequisites `P1.12` (Introduction to pandas) and `P1.13` (Columns and tables), referenced briefly as context. `P1.14` is the final lesson in the `python-for-ai` module.
- **Out of scope (deliberately left to other lessons):** `.loc` / `.iloc`, `.query()`, `.isin()`, `.str` accessors, `|` / `~` operators, `.reset_index()`, `NaN` handling, in-place mutation (`SettingWithCopyWarning`), sorting, and grouping.
