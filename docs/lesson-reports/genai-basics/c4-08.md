## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Running example:** Chose Mina’s Neighborhood Bakery checking tree-nut ingredients (`Almond Croissant`, `Walnut Brownie`, `Oat Muffin`) because it requires no arithmetic or domain prerequisite, makes 2-hop forward chaining intuitive (`Almond Paste -> Almonds -> Tree Nut`), and makes the stakes of missing facts and hybrid guardrails concrete.
- **Image provider fallback:** Followed `tooling.yaml` order (`agy-gemini` first, `codex` fallback). `agy-gemini` hit `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (~5h quota reset) on `rule-chaining` and `knowledge-graph` (while succeeding on the initial `hybrid-system` image); `codex` generated `rule-chaining.webp`, `knowledge-graph.webp`, and the Round 2 regeneration of `hybrid-system.webp`.
