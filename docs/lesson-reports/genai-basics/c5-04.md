## 2. Curriculum alignment

- **Learner outcome:** Simple Bayes example; rare events and false positives (Chapter 5: Data and Evaluation, Lesson 5.4, Deep)
- **Misconception addressed:** "A positive test means very likely true" — explicitly contrasted by separating the detection rate (chance of a positive test *if* blighted, 90%) from the updated belief (chance of blight *if* the test is positive, 8.3%).
- **Prerequisites and adjacent lessons:** Needs `5.3` (Probability and uncertainty). Adjacent to `5.5` (Training, validation and test sets) and `5.8` (Accuracy, precision and recall).
- **Out of scope (deliberately left to other lessons):** Probability calibration (`5.3`), full confusion-matrix metric taxonomy and threshold trade-offs (`5.8`), and formal continuous probability distributions (`M5.1`).

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Natural frequencies over symbolic algebra:** Used expected counts out of 1,000 cases throughout (`9 true positives` vs `99 false positives`) rather than symbolic Bayes notation so beginners can verify every ratio directly.
- **Two explainer images with two-card bottom band:** Generated `belief-update-flow.webp` and `rare-event-counts.webp` using `skills/write-web-lesson/references/educational-image-prompt.md` and `scripts/fill_image_prompt.py`, keeping both the `Analogy` and `Key takeaways` cards in the bottom band as required by the repo image standard. Verified every quoted label, arrow direction, and 390px readability before converting to WebP.
- **Conditional independence wording:** Explicitly qualified the second-test readout as requiring errors independent of the first test *given whether the event is truly present* (addressed from Plan Review Round 1).
