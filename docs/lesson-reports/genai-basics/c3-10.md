## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Two-level stakes scale (low vs. higher) + stop-signal-first rule:** In Plan Review Round 1, Codex noted a gap between "meaningful" and "high" stakes when a task is hard to check. Simplifying stakes to `low` vs. `higher` and routing any stop signal (hard to check, skill practice, or private/personal care) to `Do myself` made the decision criterion complete and unambiguous across every worked example, lab task, and quiz option.
- **Familiar broad snack categories instead of grocery aisles:** In Content Review Round 1, Codex noted that having a snack list does not by itself let Priya verify an unfamiliar store's aisle layout. Switching Task 1, the worked example, and the quiz to familiar food categories ("fruit, baked goods, and drinks") made checkability self-contained.
- **Image generation fallback to Codex:** `agy-gemini` (first in `tooling.yaml`) failed on both images with `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` on `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (quota reset ~5 hours). Fell back to `codex` (second in `tooling.yaml`), visually inspected both PNGs, converted them to 1200×800 WebP, and deleted the source PNGs.
