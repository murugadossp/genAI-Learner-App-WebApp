  1. **1. Find the missing group:** Learners audit three illustrative samples (*Metro Bus Voice Helper*, *City Bike-Share Demand*, *School Homework Helper*), each grouped by one non-overlapping criterion, inspect counts and visual bars, and click the missing or scarce group to receive immediate, specific feedback in a live region.
  2. **2. Test "more data":** Learners compare three Metro Bus Voice Helper collection strategies—Baseline (1,000 office clips, 1 of 4 settings present), Add 9,000 office clips (10,000 total, still 1 of 4 settings present), and Add 600 targeted clips (1,600 total, 4 of 4 settings present)—observing how total count grows without fixing blind spots unless collection targets missing settings.
- **Knowledge check:** Shared `<Check>` component (`#check`), correct option at index `2` (3rd option). Scenario: city library book-recommendation model serving all age groups across print and audiobooks, where the team collected 20,000 mobile audiobook app records (mostly adult commuters) and plans to add 30,000 more from the same app.
  - Option 0 wrong feedback: total count alone is not the main problem described; the issue is who is left out.
  - Option 1 wrong feedback: data cleaning (Lesson 5.2) will not add missing print borrowers, children, teens, and seniors.
  - Option 3 wrong feedback: a representative sample does not require exact census percentages; oversampling is common, whereas leaving whole groups out creates blind spots.
