## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **10-step battery limit + `+4` tap bonus:** Adjusted from the initial 12-step / `+2` draft after Plan Review Round 1 showed a hybrid tap+seedling route could outscore the dry loop (`+16` vs `+12`). With a 10-step limit and `+4` per tap visit, the dry tap loop (`+20`) strictly outscores the best seedling-watering route (`+18`) and the direct route (`+10`).
- **Image generation fallback:** Per `references/tooling.yaml`, `agy-gemini` was attempted first for all three explainer images and returned `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (`QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` on `gemini-3.1-flash-image`). Fell back to `codex` (second provider in `tooling.yaml`) using the identical `fill_image_prompt.py` prompts with both `Analogy` and `Key takeaways` cards. All three images were visually inspected before WebP conversion (`1200x800`, 68–82 KB each).
- **Schematic greenhouse icon in Image 1:** Image 1's environment card depicts a schematic greenhouse grid icon rather than the exact playable 4×3 tile layout; explicitly noted as schematic in both the alt text and `<figcaption>`.
