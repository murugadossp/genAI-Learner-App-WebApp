## 2. Curriculum alignment

- **Learner outcome:** Training data has a cutoff and coverage before it is incomplete; tools or supplied context can add newer facts.
- **Misconception addressed:** It knows everything before the cutoff and nothing after.
- **Prerequisites and adjacent lessons:** needs 2.3 (c2-03); neighbours c2-06 (context window), c2-07 (making things up).
- **Out of scope:** how retrieval or RAG works (chapter 8), tool calling (chapter 9), specific provider cutoff dates.

## 3. What was built

- **Files added:** `src/lessons/genai-basics/c2-08/{meta.json,Lesson.tsx,CutoffLab.tsx,lesson.module.css}`, `tests/web/c2-08.spec.ts`, this report.
- **Interaction:** a slider moves the training cutoff over an invented tram-line timeline; each event shows not in training, thin or well covered. The learner asks one of four questions (fare cap, station name, last stop, "what month is it today") with nothing added, an old notice, or a current notice. The answer panel labels the result as matches today, out of date, unsteady, or no basis, and shows the toy-world fact. Reset restores defaults.
- **Knowledge check:** correct option is position 2 of 4 (index 1). Wrong 1: chat does not update weights. Wrong 3: coverage before the cutoff is uneven and facts change. Wrong 4: tone is not evidence of checking.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- Used a fictional tram line so no real-world fact can go stale in a lesson about staleness.
- Dropped the six-month window to three months so the slider range can reach "well covered" for the latest fare change.
- Live region announces only on question or source change, not on every slider step, to stay restrained.
- No expanded workspace, so no focus or Escape handling applies.
