- **Interaction:** `ThresholdLab` lets learners move a cutoff slider (`0.10`–`0.95`, step `0.05`, default `0.50`) or click quick cutoffs (`Flag more (0.20)`, `Default (0.50)`, `Flag less (0.75)`, `Reset to 0.50`) on Sam’s six invented bookstore emails with fixed spam scores. Learners see each email’s score bar, shifting cutoff marker, Sam’s reference label (`Wanted` / `Spam`), the filter’s decision (`Inbox` / `Spam`), and live counts of flagged mail, inbox mail, false alarms, and missed spam—making the overlapping-score tradeoff directly observable.
- **Knowledge check:** Scenario on a separate invented batch where Sam’s filter gives a customer’s `Wanted` order email a `0.64` spam score; Sam raises the cutoff from `0.50` to `0.75` without retraining and reapplies the rule. Correct option is at index `1` (second option). Wrong feedback:
  - Option 0: explains that moving the cutoff does not recalculate the `0.64` score and gives no guarantee that both mistake counts drop to zero.
  - Option 2: explains that retraining is not needed to reapply a new cutoff rule to an existing score.
  - Option 3: confirms the email stays `0.64` and moves to Inbox, but points out the tradeoff direction is reversed.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Overlapping scores in running example (`0.66` Wanted promo > `0.52` Spam pitch):** Chosen instead of perfectly separable scores so learners discover that no single cutoff yields zero mistakes and that threshold choice reflects which mistake costs more.
- **Reference labels (`Wanted` / `Spam`) separated from filter decisions (`Inbox` / `Spam`):** Adopted after Plan Review Round 1 so `Inbox` is not overloaded as both ground truth and destination.
- **Image 2 regeneration fallback to Codex:** `agy` generated Image 1 (`score-to-label.webp`) and initial Image 2 cleanly; when regenerating Image 2 (`threshold-tradeoff.webp`) after Content Review Round 1, `agy` returned `HTTP 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`, so generation fell back to `codex` per `references/tooling.yaml`.
- **Content Review Rounds 2 and 3 provider:** Codex reached its usage limit during Image 2 v3 generation, so Content Review Rounds 2 and 3 used the next provider in `references/tooling.yaml` (`agy-gemini`: `gemini-4-neon-medium`, read-only `--mode plan`).
