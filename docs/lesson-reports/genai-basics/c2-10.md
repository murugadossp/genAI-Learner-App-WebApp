## 5. Decisions and deviations

- The ordinary-model "show steps" case is deliberately a case where steps do not help, and the lesson and lab say that real models often benefit, to avoid teaching that step prompting is useless.
- The summary view omits the discarded first attempt to show that a displayed text need not mirror the work.
- Live region announces after a short delay on question, mode or slider change.
- Codex review iteration 1: softened the "one chance" opening and "improves the odds"; labelled the toy trace as an inspection-only view (it is intentionally always visible and separate from "what the app shows").
- Added an explainer diagram (quick reply vs reasoning model, now the pet puzzle) generated with Codex image generation and embedded with `next/image`; its caption says the example is invented. Its text is small at 390px (checked once in dark, by eye). The lab instructions became a numbered list for easier following.
- After feedback that the lesson was hard to follow: replaced the arithmetic ferry example with a non-calculation logic puzzle, added a plain-language walkthrough section ("example") before the lab, renamed the lab header to start with "Mix and match", and regenerated the diagram for the new example.
- Codex review iteration 3: the clue "Asha is allergic to fur" did not strictly rule out a furry pet, so the puzzle was reworded to "Asha is allergic to fur, so she cannot own a pet with fur" in the walkthrough, lab and regenerated diagram.
- After feedback that the reasoning text was too complex: rewrote the opening with a student analogy, shortened the walkthrough and the scripted trace into plain short sentences (read the clues, make a guess, check and fix, check again), set the puzzle in a quote block with numbered clues, and regenerated the diagram with the same step wording.
- No expanded workspace, so no focus or Escape handling applies.
