Correct option is at index `1` ("A 3-minute gap does not mean the line is broken—regression gives an estimate, and real deliveries often differ from the line because of factors beyond distance alone.").
  - Wrong option 0 feedback: explains that a fitted line summarizes the overall trend and does not have to pass through every data point.
  - Wrong option 2 feedback: explains that copying a single past ride ignores the overall pattern and can be thrown off by one unusual trip.
  - Wrong option 3 feedback: explains that predicting a 30 km ride is extrapolation far beyond the 1–6 km range, where each extra kilometer may not add the same extra minutes.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Fixed chart y-domain (`−8` to `60` min) in `RegressionLab`:** Chosen instead of dynamic y-axis rescaling after Content Review Round 3 identified that dynamic rescaling can jump a dot mid-drag. The fixed domain mathematically bounds all possible OLS predictions (`−6.48` to `58.48` min) for any valid point configuration in `4–48` min.
- **Schematic explainer images:** Both generated infographics illustrate the concepts schematically rather than plotting Sam’s exact 6 coordinates; captions and alt text state this explicitly and direct learners to the table and interactive chart for Sam’s exact 6 deliveries.
- **Simplified lab metrics (`Estimate at 3.5 km`, `Slope (min per km)`, `Average gap`):** Omitted the y-intercept ("base") readout after Plan Review noted that the `Delay outlier` preset produces a `−2 min` intercept that beginners could misread as a real delivery time.
