## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Four decision lenses with safety-first priority:** Instead of presenting the four categories as rigid mutually exclusive bins (flagged in Plan Review R1), the lesson frames them as four decision lenses checked in order—safety first, because a single note can be both sensitive and relevant or act as both context and constraint.
- **Two focused WebP diagrams:** Created `prompt-inputs.webp` (facts + constraints -> AI model -> focused draft) and `leave-out.webp` (filtering out distracting and sensitive notes before sending) rather than one compound diagram, keeping each image to one clear idea.
- **Hover contrast override for pressed buttons:** Applied explicit `button.blockBtn[aria-pressed="true"], button.blockBtn.blockBtnActive:not(:disabled):hover` rules in `lesson.module.css` to prevent global `app/studio.css` hover rules from lowering contrast, verified with a computed contrast ratio check (>= 4.5:1) in Playwright.
