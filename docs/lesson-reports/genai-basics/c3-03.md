- **Interaction:** `InstructionLab` ("Harbor Helper — Instruction Lab") lets learners compare three system-instruction presets (`No shop instruction`, `Mechanic role + rules`, `Safety coach role + rules`) against two customer messages (`Normal question` vs. `Rule-pushing message`). Learners observe the assembled inputs, the simulated reply (including a clearly badged `Simulated slip` when pushed by a conflicting message), and two side-by-side readouts: **What the instruction steered** and **What it did not guarantee**, plus `Reset` and polite `role="status"` announcements.
- **Knowledge check:** Harbor Bike Shop assistant configured as a "master bicycle mechanic with 20 years of experience" with rules "Always keep replies to three steps and never guess repair prices" and no connection to the shop's price list; customer asks how much a chain repair costs, and the assistant replies with four steps and guesses "$20". Correct answer at index `2` (3rd option).
  - Option 0 feedback: explains system instructions are soft steering, not unbreakable locks, so receiving them does not prevent slips.
  - Option 1 feedback: explains a stronger role title only shifts framing and tone and cannot connect a price list or add new factual training.
  - Option 3 feedback: explains a customer's chat message does not retrain a model; the reply slipped during that turn.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Running example:** Used an invented neighborhood bicycle shop ("Harbor Bike Shop" / "Harbor Helper") and a single slipped-chain question so beginners need no math, coding, or domain jargon.
- **Image regeneration fallback:** `agy-gemini` generated `role-steering-limits.webp` and the first version of `system-vs-user.webp`, then hit `HTTP 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` on `gemini-3.1-flash-image` when regenerating `system-vs-user.webp` after Content Review Round 1. Followed `references/tooling.yaml` `image_generation.order` and used `codex` (`codex exec`) for `system-vs-user.webp` v2.
- **Active button hover contrast:** Removed CSS `transition` on `button.choiceBtn` and applied `!important` rules on `button.choiceBtn[aria-pressed="true"]:not(:disabled):hover` to prevent `app/studio.css` hover rules from dropping contrast; verified with a WCAG >= 4.5 computed contrast assertion in `tests/web/c3-03.spec.ts`.
