## 2. Curriculum alignment

- **Learner outcome:** Give targeted feedback; compare versions with the original requirements.
- **Misconception addressed:** Polish equals improvement (smoother or livelier wording does not fix missing details or broken constraints).
- **Prerequisites and adjacent lessons:** Prerequisite `3.1` (`c3-01`, Giving clear goals and instructions); adjacent `3.6` (`c3-06`, Breaking complex tasks into steps) and `3.8` (`c3-08`, Checking facts, sources and generated code).
- **Out of scope (deliberately left to other lessons):** Writing an initial prompt from scratch with audience and goal (`3.1`), selecting background context and constraints (`3.2`), system instructions and roles (`3.3`), few-shot examples (`3.4`), structured JSON/table schemas (`3.5`), splitting complex tasks into checkable subtasks (`3.6`), and verifying outside facts, sources, or generated code (`3.8`, noted explicitly in the lesson's scope note).

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Running example choice:** Chose Sam’s Community Book Swap Flyer with 3 binary, self-contained requirements (swap rule, exact time and place, ≤3 short plain sentences) requiring no arithmetic or external domain knowledge. Considered a technical email or code example, rejected to keep the lesson beginner-friendly and avoid overlapping Lesson 3.8.
- **Image provider fallback:** Followed `skills/write-web-lesson/references/tooling.yaml` order (`[agy-gemini, codex]`). `agy` returned `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (`QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` on `gemini-3.1-flash-image`, ~5h reset), so fell back to `codex` (`codex exec --sandbox workspace-write`), visually inspected every generated PNG, converted to 1200×800 WebP, and deleted the PNG originals.
- **Hover contrast safeguard:** Styled active `aria-pressed="true"` choice buttons with `!important` overrides in `lesson.module.css` to prevent `app/studio.css`'s `button:not(.button):not(:disabled):hover` from resetting active button backgrounds to `var(--panel)`, and asserted ≥4.5:1 computed contrast on hover in Playwright across all viewports and themes.
