- **Interaction:** `HideAndGuessLab` lets the learner pick one of three 8-word raw sentences (`Soup recipe`, `Weather note`, `Leaving home`), toggle between `Fill in the blank` (masked prediction) and `Predict next word`, click one of three highlighted words to hide it, and guess from four candidate words. Choosing a guess reveals the self-supplied target from the original sentence, states the shared single-step comparison rule, and explains how visible context affects plausibility in the active mode. Includes a `Reset lab` control and an explicit disclosure that sentences, candidates, and explanations are pre-written illustrative examples.
- **Knowledge check:** Weather archive scenario with millions of unlabelled daily forecast notes; correct option at index `1` ("Mask one word in each forecast note and check the model’s prediction against the masked word from that same note."). Wrong feedback:
  - Index `0`: Skipping human tags alone is not enough—grouping notes without predicting a target is clustering (Lesson 4.4), whereas self-supervised learning derives a target to predict.
  - Index `2`: When meteorologists attach outside category tags like "storm" or "clear," that is supervised learning (Lesson 4.1)—even if the training loop runs automatically afterward.
  - Index `3`: Self-supervised learning checks predictions against the raw text itself, not against real-world weather outcomes or factual truth.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Two explainer diagrams (`self-supervised-target.webp` and `two-hiding-tasks.webp`):** Generated with the 3-band pastel card layout including both bottom-left `Analogy` and bottom-right `Key takeaways` cards. Keeps one core concept per image so text stays legible at 390px.
- **Three selectable content words per sentence in `HideAndGuessLab`:** Rather than letting learners click stop-words like `"the"` with generic feedback, three content words per sentence have authored candidate lists and mode-aware plausibility (`kindNext` for `"toaster"` after `"Pack a"`), while badges show both the full-sentence pair count and `(3 selectable here)`.
- **Hidden word displays as `[hidden]` in the sentence strip:** Avoids leaking the target word before the learner guesses on secondary presets (`Weather note`, `Leaving home`).
- **Round 4 P3 note deferred:** Content review Round 4 was clean at P2+ (reaching two clean rounds in a row) and noted one minor P3 (`"texture word"` vs `"how the carrots feel"` when `"soft"` is hidden). Kept as-is without re-opening the verified build.
