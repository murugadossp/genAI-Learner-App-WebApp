- **Interaction:** `CleaningLab` (`data-testid="cleaning-lab"`) lets learners fix Nadia's 6-row meal-delivery table in two steps: (1) toggle format corrections for D2 (`"town centre"` → `"Town Centre"`) and D3 (`"20 mins"` → `20`) and mark D4's impossible `-15` as missing, individually or via **Fix all formats & errors**; (2) switch among five missing-value strategies (**Keep visible & flag missing**, **Drop incomplete rows**, **Fill with overall average**, **Fill with Hilltop average**, **Fill with 0 (pitfall)**) and watch rows kept, Hilltop share, overall average vs 30-min target, Hilltop average, and an `aria-live="polite"` explanation update live, with **Reset table** restoring the raw state.
- **Knowledge check:** Town library daily visitor table (branch-day rows for Central and Westside) where Westside's door counter loses power on two rainy Fridays while the branch stays open, and the analyst drops blank rows before summing monthly totals. Correct answer at index `2` (dropping blank rows omits two open Westside Fridays while keeping every Central day, which can under-count Westside's monthly visits and skew the decision on evening hours). Wrong feedback: `0` explains why omitting two Westside Fridays is not neutral; `1` explains why filling `0` treats a power outage as zero visitors and lowers Westside's average; `3` distinguishes format mismatches from missing values.
