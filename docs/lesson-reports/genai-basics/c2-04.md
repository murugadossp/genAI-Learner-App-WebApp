- **Knowledge check:**
  - Question: Why can't you turn a raw base model into a reliable assistant simply by prefixing every prompt with 'You are a helpful assistant'?
  - Correct option: Position 2 of 4 (index 1): "Because a prompt prefix only adds more context to complete; without instruction tuning, the model may still continue the text as a list, forum thread, or script rather than following the instruction."
  - Wrong 1 feedback: Base models produce natural language fluently—in fact, they are trained on billions of pages of natural text. The limitation is that they complete text patterns rather than obeying instructions.
  - Wrong 3 feedback: Base models handle multiple languages depending on their pretraining data. Language is not the reason prompt prefixes fail to create an assistant persona.
  - Wrong 4 feedback: Base models process the entire context window. They do not discard text before punctuation.
