The live prompt text, dynamic word count, clear-part counter (`0 of 3` to `3 of 3`), invented illustrative reply, and "What the assistant had to guess" list update immediately, showing that filler padding increases word count without reducing ambiguity while naming Action, Audience, and Outcome removes guesses one by one.   - Option 1 feedback: explains that action and audience are named, but the outcome is missing (bring a bike, donate parts, or volunteer?).
  - Option 3 feedback: explains that while specific, a ten-paragraph history article asks for the wrong action and outcome for recruiting Saturday mechanics.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Three single-idea WebP diagrams instead of one compound diagram:** Separate visuals for vague-prompt branching (`vague-guessing.webp`), the three-part convergence (`three-parts.webp`), and length-vs-clarity (`length-vs-clarity.webp`) keep each frame scannable at 390px mobile width.
- **Dynamic word count & explicit "clear parts" rubric:** After Gate 1 review noted that "write something" is literally a verb, we defined **Action** as a specific deliverable (not "write something") and counted `0 of 3 clear parts` through `3 of 3 clear parts` consistently across prose, images, and `PromptLab`.
- **Gate 2 round cap:** Round 4 surfaced a P2 detail in the quiz ("two-hour" shift duration not established in the scenario). We fixed it immediately and re-ran all checks; because the 4-round cap was reached without two consecutive clean rounds *after* the Round 4 edit, this is noted honestly in Section 6 below.
